Vain Playbook - third-party software

The playbook includes the code below. Each part keeps its own licence, printed in full further down.

  PS-SFTA (Set-FTA) - MIT License - https://github.com/DanysysTeam/PS-SFTA
      Executables\SetFileTypeAssociation.ps1: sets 7-Zip as the default for archive files.

  RunAsTI (LeanAndMean) - MIT License - https://github.com/AveYo/LeanAndMean
      Executables\RunAsTi.reg: the "Run as TrustedInstaller" context menu entry.

The software the playbook downloads during the install (Vain Tools, the Visual C++ runtimes,
DirectX, 7-Zip and the browser you choose) is not part of the playbook and comes with its own
licence from its publisher.
